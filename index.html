08 / Process

My Design Approach

A non-linear, user-centered framework engineered for clarity and repeatability.

01
Discovery

Understand

Explore the problem, users, and context. Conduct stakeholder interviews and analyze competitive alternatives before formulating any solution.

02
Clarity

Define

Organize insights, user needs, and product requirements. Synthesize research into personas and concrete user task lists.

03
Ideation

Explore

Develop user flows, wireframes, and potential solutions. Validate architectural routes fast without attachment to a single concept.

04
Execution

Design

Create responsive interfaces, visual hierarchy, and reusable components. Build with atomic design principles in Figma.

05
Iteration

Refine

Use feedback, usability considerations, and iteration to improve the experience. Eliminate visual friction and confusing labels.

06
Delivery

Handoff

Prepare designs and supporting details for development collaboration. Redline specs, export assets, and partner during QA.
