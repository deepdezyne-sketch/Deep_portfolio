07 / NARRATIVE BLUEPRINT

Standard 13-Stage Case Study Architecture

Every project is documented with deliberate rigor from problem identification to technical handoff.

STAGE 01

Project Introduction

Client context, platform scope, and high-level role definition.

STAGE 02

Problem Statement

Core friction points, usability hurdles, and baseline metrics.

STAGE 03

Target Users

Behavioral segmentations, demographics, and primary pain points.

STAGE 04

Research & Discovery

User interviews, competitive auditing, and qualitative findings.

STAGE 05

Information Architecture

Sitemaps, structural taxonomy, and content grouping schemes.

STAGE 06

Low-Fi Wireframes

Rapid structural sketches testing spatial priority without color.

STAGE 07

Visual Design System

Typography scales, color tokens, and responsive component libraries.

STAGE 08

Hi-Fi Prototype

Fully interactive flows capturing transitions and real state logic.

STAGE 09

Key Design Decisions

Detailed rationales behind tradeoffs, layouts, and accessibility.

STAGE 10

Usability Testing

Task success rates, user friction recordings, and iterative tweaks.

STAGE 11

Developer Handoff

Figma inspection notes, auto-layout definitions, and token exports.

STAGE 12

Outcomes & Impact

Conversion shifts, retention gains, and qualitative client remarks.

STAGE 13 • SYNTHESIS lightbulb

Learnings & Future Iteration

What was learned from real user behavior post-launch, how constraints shaped the outcome, and what would be explored next with more data.

08 / PROCESS

My Design Approach

A non-linear, user-centered framework engineered for clarity and repeatability.

01 Discovery

Understand

Explore the problem, users, and context. Conduct stakeholder interviews and analyze competitive alternatives before formulating any solution.

02 Clarity

Define

Organize insights, user needs, and product requirements. Synthesize research into personas and concrete user task lists.

03 Ideation

Explore

Develop user flows, wireframes, and potential solutions. Validate architectural routes fast without attachment to a single concept.

04 Execution

Design

Create responsive interfaces, visual hierarchy, and reusable components. Build with atomic design principles in Figma.

05 Iteration

Refine

Use feedback, usability considerations, and iteration to improve the experience. Eliminate visual friction and confusing labels.

06 Delivery

Handoff

Prepare designs and supporting details for development collaboration. Redline specs, export assets, and partner during engineering QA.
